Our story

Amperage started when a master electrician got tired of being called back to fix other contractors' shortcuts — panels that technically worked but never would have passed a real inspection, wiring that was "good enough" until it wasn't. So he built a company with one non-negotiable rule: if it wouldn't pass inspection, it doesn't leave the panel that way.

Today Amperage handles panel upgrades, EV charger installs, lighting, and repairs across the Denver metro, with {{ site.business.jobs_completed }}+ jobs completed and a callback rate near zero.
