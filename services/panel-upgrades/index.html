Services / Panel Upgrades

A panel sized for how your home actually uses power

Most panel failures aren't dramatic — they're a 100-amp panel quietly overloaded by an EV charger, a hot tub, and a home addition it was never sized for.

Book a Service

What's included

  • Load calculation based on your actual and planned electrical usage, not a generic sizing chart
  • Full panel and meter base replacement, including grounding and bonding brought up to current code
  • Permit pulled and inspection scheduled as part of the job, not an add-on
  • Circuit labeling and a clean, organized final panel — no guessing which breaker does what
  • Coordination with your utility for any required service disconnect
  • Same-day power restoration in nearly all cases

Typical timeline

A standard panel upgrade typically takes 4–8 hours on the day of installation, plus utility coordination and permit lead time that can add 1–2 weeks depending on your jurisdiction.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if I need a panel upgrade?

Frequently tripped breakers, a panel still using fuses, or planning an EV charger or major addition are the most common signs. We'll give you an honest read during a free assessment.

Will we be without power during the upgrade?

Yes, typically for a few hours during the actual swap — we schedule this for a time that works for your household and coordinate with the utility in advance.

Do you handle the permit and inspection?

Yes, start to finish — you don't have to coordinate with the city or the utility yourself.
