Services / Lighting Design & Installation

Lighting wired in, not just screwed in

A beautiful fixture on an undersized or poorly placed circuit is still a compromise. We plan the wiring and the fixture together.

Book a Service

What's included

  • Layout planning for recessed lighting that accounts for joist spacing and existing wiring, not just an even grid
  • New circuits run where needed, rather than overloading an existing one to save an hour of labor
  • Dimmer and smart-switch compatibility checked before installation, not discovered after
  • Pendant, chandelier, and fixture mounting rated for the actual weight of your fixture
  • Landscape and exterior lighting on weatherproof, code-compliant circuits
  • Final walkthrough to confirm every switch does what you expect

Typical timeline

A standard room of recessed lighting typically takes 1 day; whole-home lighting projects run 2–4 days depending on scope. Landscape lighting projects typically complete in a single day.

Frequently asked questions

Can you install lighting without opening up our ceiling?

In many cases yes, using techniques that minimize drywall work — we'll give you an honest assessment of what your specific project requires.

Do you work with smart lighting systems?

Yes — we regularly wire for and install smart switches, dimmers, and lighting control systems alongside standard fixtures.

Can you match new lighting to our existing electrical setup?

Yes — we'll assess your existing circuits and let you know if any need upgrading to support new fixtures before we begin.
