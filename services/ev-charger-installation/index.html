Services / EV Charger Installation

A charger install that starts with your panel, not the charger

The most common EV charger installation mistake is sizing the circuit to the charger without first checking whether the panel can actually support it.

Book a Service

What's included

  • A panel capacity assessment before quoting the install — some homes need a panel upgrade first, and we'll tell you honestly if yours does
  • Dedicated 240V circuit sized to your specific charger and vehicle
  • Charger mounting at a location that makes sense for your garage or driveway layout, not just the shortest wire run
  • Permit pulled and inspection scheduled as part of the job
  • Compatible with all major charger brands — we can also help you choose one if you haven't purchased yet
  • Load management setup where a panel upgrade isn't practical

Typical timeline

A standard Level 2 charger installation typically takes 3–5 hours when the panel has available capacity. Installs requiring a sub-panel or panel upgrade run longer — we'll give you an exact estimate after the panel assessment.

Frequently asked questions

What if my panel doesn't have room for a new circuit?

We can often add a sub-panel or use a load management device instead of a full panel upgrade — we'll walk you through the tradeoffs and costs of each.

Can you install a charger for any EV brand?

Yes — Level 2 chargers use a standard circuit regardless of vehicle brand, though connector types vary and we'll make sure you get the right one.

Do I need a permit for a home EV charger?

In most Denver metro jurisdictions, yes — we handle the permit and inspection as part of every install.
