What's included

  • Flat-rate diagnostic visit — you know the cost before we touch a single wire
  • Systematic circuit tracing to find the actual source of flickering lights, tripped breakers, or dead outlets
  • Repair options presented with honest tradeoffs, not just the most expensive fix

Typical timeline

Most diagnostic visits identify the issue within 1–2 hours. Simple repairs are often completed the same visit; more involved repairs are scheduled with an exact quote once the cause is confirmed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the diagnostic visit really flat-rate?

Yes — you'll know the diagnostic cost upfront, and any repair work is quoted separately before we proceed.

What if the problem is intermittent and doesn't show up during the visit?

We use the same systematic tracing process either way — most intermittent issues have a root cause we can identify even when the symptom isn't actively occurring.

Do you offer emergency repair visits?

Yes — call our main line for urgent issues like sparking outlets, burning smells, or a total loss of power.
